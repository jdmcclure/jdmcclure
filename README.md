## What I Work On

My work sits at the intersection of data engineering and storytelling. I design and build reporting solutions that help to understand data, track performance, and make better decisions primarily using the Microsoft data stack (Power BI and Fabric).

**Current focus areas:**

- **Power BI** — Building semantic models, authoring DAX measures, and designing dashboards centered on research award data and related institutional reporting
- **Microsoft Fabric** — Developing unified analytics pipelines, notebooks, and lakehouses to support data workflows at scale
- **Data Visualization** — Crafting visuals that communicate clearly without sacrificing nuance; the goal is always insight, not decoration

## Background

My path into data started in crime analysis and federal reporting compliance, seven years of work that sharpened both my technical instincts and my appreciation for getting details right. That foundation carried me into my current focus on research award data and research training and compliance reporting.
